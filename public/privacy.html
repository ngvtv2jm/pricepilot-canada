Privacy notice

Version 1 · Updated 2026-11-05

What we collect and why

Account email and authentication information support sign-in and recovery. Shopping lists, price watches and in-app notifications support your chosen features. Support messages, request status and timestamps let administrators answer requests. Administrative changes create limited audit records. Do not send sensitive personal information in support messages.

Device storage and location

Guest lists and the first three characters of a postal code you choose to save are stored in your browser. Authentication session tokens are also kept in browser storage by the authentication SDK. If you choose Use my location in the branch finder, your browser asks permission to share device coordinates with this page. Distances are calculated on the device. PricePine does not save or transmit the coordinates or send postal searches to a geocoder. Device data can remain until you remove it or clear browser storage. Device storage controls in the app let you remove guest lists and the saved postal code separately from your account.

Service providers and external requests

Bolt hosting and the project’s database/authentication infrastructure process requests to operate the service. Public catalog feeds and some cached images are fetched from GitHub. Some product images may fall back to retailer or image-provider servers. These services can receive your IP address and request metadata. Retailer links open independent services with their own privacy practices. We do not send private shopping lists to the public catalog repository.

Retention

Account lists and watches remain until removed or the account is deleted. Support records remain for handling and follow-up; administrators must review necessity and applicable legal obligations before retention or deletion. Audit records and provider backups may have separate retention periods. A formal retention schedule and provider backup deletion periods have not yet been confirmed; this is not a promise of immediate or automatic erasure.

Access, correction and deletion

My account provides an export of your account records and a tracked request form for access, correction, privacy questions and deletion. The export does not include provider security logs or backups; request assistance for additional information. Deletion is reviewed by an administrator and requires privileged provider action. You can remove individual list items and watches immediately. Signing out ends your session but does not delete your account.

International processing

Service providers may process information outside your province or country. Hosting regions, processor terms and applicable transfer requirements must be confirmed by the operator. No claim is made that all data remains in Canada.

Optional processing and your choices

No marketing analytics, advertising trackers or marketing subscriptions are enabled in this release. No optional tracking consent is requested for features that do not exist. Any future optional tracking or marketing will need an appropriate notice and consent mechanism before activation. Account data is used to provide the features you request.

Contact and changes

Use the privacy request form in My account. The public privacy email and verified legal operator identity are pending publication. The version and update date below identify this notice. Applicable privacy obligations, retention periods and cross-border arrangements require operator and legal review.
