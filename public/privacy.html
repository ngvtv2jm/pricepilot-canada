Privacy notice

PricePine uses your email and authentication details to provide an account. Account shopping lists and price watches are stored in the project database and restricted to your account by access policies.

On your device

Guest shopping lists and your voluntarily supplied postal code are kept in browser local storage. Authentication session information is managed by our authentication provider. We do not request precise GPS location.

Service providers

The app uses Bolt hosting and its database/authentication infrastructure. These providers process service requests needed to operate the app. Product images may be served through its storage service.

Your choices

You can remove list items and price watches in the app, sign out, and clear device storage. Clearing browser storage removes guest lists from that browser. Signed-in users can download their account data and submit privacy or account deletion requests from My account. Requests are reviewed by an administrator and do not immediately delete the account.

Current stage

No advertising trackers, paid subscription billing or marketing analytics are enabled. Retailer links may be labelled affiliate or sponsored. Following a retailer link opens that retailer’s website, which has its own privacy practices.
