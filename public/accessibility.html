Accessibility statement

Version 1 · Updated 2026-11-05

Our approach

PricePine aims to make shopping comparisons usable with a keyboard, assistive technology and small screens. Improvements include labelled inputs, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, responsive layouts, text alternatives and reduced-motion support.

Known limits

This statement is not a certification of WCAG conformance. Independent screen-reader, keyboard and device testing remains necessary. Wide comparison tables can scroll horizontally; product information may be incomplete. External retailer websites are outside our control.

Report a barrier

Use Contact & support to describe the page, device, assistive technology and issue. Avoid sharing sensitive information. The operator will review the request; no guaranteed response time has been established.
