Cookies & device storage

Version 1 · Updated 2026-11-05

Current storage

PricePine uses local storage for your guest shopping list, voluntarily saved postal area and authentication session. Hosting or authentication providers may use necessary security mechanisms. This release does not install optional analytics or advertising trackers.

Your controls

Open Device storage controls to clear the guest list and postal code. Sign out to end your account session on this browser. Browser settings can remove all site storage, which may sign you out and remove unsaved device lists. This does not delete account records.

Future optional tracking

If optional analytics, advertising or marketing tools are introduced, the operator must assess consent requirements and provide appropriate controls before those tools run. Continued browsing is not treated as consent.
