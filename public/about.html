How PricePine works

Version 1 · Updated 2026-11-05

A clearer view of your weekly shop

PricePine Canada is a free shopping-planning and price-comparison service for shoppers in Canada. Compare the same product and package size, then confirm the final price directly with the retailer. We do not sell groceries, process payments or fulfil orders.

How comparisons work

Dated website observations are separate from verified branch offers. Website prices do not confirm physical store stock or local pricing. Branch basket totals only include eligible CAD offers checked within 48 hours and not expired. Membership prices are optional. Taxes, deposits, shipping and travel costs are excluded.

Your lists and watches

Guest lists remain on your device. Accounts provide private shopping lists, supported product price watches and in-app notifications. Email and push price alerts are not enabled.

Independence

No retailer affiliation is implied. Affiliate and sponsored links, where configured, are labelled and do not change lowest-price rankings. We do not guarantee savings or complete market coverage.
