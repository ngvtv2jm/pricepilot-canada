A clearer view of your weekly shop.

PricePilot Canada helps you organize a shopping list and compare eligible prices for the same product and package size.

What a comparison includes

Comparisons use Canadian-dollar offers, checked within the last 48 hours and not past their expiry. Member prices are optional. A complete basket requires a current offer for every unchecked item. Taxes, deposits, delivery fees and travel costs are not included.

Availability

Retailer source connections are pending. No retailer partnership or live price coverage is claimed. A product’s price may change before checkout. Confirm price and availability with the store.

Your lists and watches

Guest lists stay on your device. Signed-in users can keep private lists and target-price watches. Watches are checked inside the app; email and push delivery are not enabled.
