Version 1 · Updated 2026-11-05

Dated observations

Retailer website observations are evidence of a listed price at the displayed check time, not a promise of a price at your nearest branch. Match the exact package, retailer conditions and membership requirements. An unverified or stale observation must not be treated as a current verified branch offer.

Commercial relationships

Configured affiliate or sponsored links are labelled. PricePine may earn a commission through affiliate links. These relationships do not determine lowest-price rankings. Retailer names and product images identify products; their appearance is not an endorsement or partnership claim.

Report a problem

Use Contact & support to report an inaccurate price, broken image, mismatched package or rights concern. Include the product URL so the operator can investigate.
